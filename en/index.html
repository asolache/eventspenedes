Why here

What a budget cannot hold

A hotel room is booked in ten minutes and forgotten in two days. What cannot be replicated is this: a landscape that has been producing for a thousand years, and people who still work it by hand. That is what a board remembers six months later.

The wine

Cava and wine with the people who make it

Not an off-the-shelf tasting: a conversation with the person who decided when to harvest. Guided cellar tastings, cava verticals and pairings built for your group, in family wineries and historic houses of the region.

The olive oil

Liquid gold, and a mill that opens its doors

Extra virgin olive oil tasting in an organic mill, with the press right there. It works all year round and surprises even those who have already done three wine tastings: almost nobody has tasted freshly milled oil.

The farmhouse

A house, not a function room

Stone farmhouses with threshing floor, porch and garden among the vines, privately yours. No hotel PA system, no other event next door: for those hours the house is yours.

The landscape

Golden hour over the vines

Montserrat behind and the sea twenty minutes away. We build the schedule around the light, because the photo your client will show afterwards is taken at seven in the evening, not at midday.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask before booking

What area do you cover?

We work across the Alt Penedès and its surroundings, based in Vilafranca del Penedès. We are about 40 minutes from Barcelona and the airport, which makes a full company day out and back in the same day perfectly possible.

Do you organise transport from Barcelona to the Penedès?

Yes. We provide private minibuses with a driver who picks the group up in Barcelona, at the hotel or at El Prat airport, takes them between wineries and farmhouses and drops them back at the hotel door. The driver stays with the group all day, so nobody has to drive after a tasting.

What group sizes do you work with?

From small groups up to around 500 people, depending on the venue. Cal Segue takes groups of up to around 30; La Torre del Gall, 350 standing or 280 seated; and Masia Torreblanca, up to 500. For each group we propose the venue and programme that fit its size.

What kind of events do you produce?

Team days and team building, conventions and kick-offs, incentives, company celebrations and festivals. We work for event agencies, for venues that want a programme, and for end clients.

Do you have your own venue or work with others?

Both. We run Cal Segue, in Torrelles de Foix, and the Castells Workshop, both together with TeamTowers. Beyond that we work with a network of partner venues across the county: farmhouses, cava cellars and wineries open to visits, hotels with meeting rooms and natural settings.

How many people does Cal Segue take?

Groups of up to around 30 people. At Cal Segue we rent the outdoor space only: the tiled terrace, the covered porch with its barbecue and the garden. For larger groups we propose venues from the network.

What activities can we do?

The «Fent Pinya» human-tower workshop, vineyard treasure hunts, cooking challenges, cava and wine tastings, outdoor activities and a DJ set. We also design bespoke activities when there is a specific objective.

Do you work with event agencies?

Yes. We do executive production on site, venue scouting and booking, and white-label activities that the agency resells under its own brand.

What languages do you work in?

Catalan, Spanish and English, both in production and in the activities with participants.

How long does a quote take?

Usually between 48 and 72 working hours from the briefing, with venue options, programme and a fixed budget.

Do you publish prices or rate cards?

There are no prices on the site: every event is quoted individually, because the cost depends on the venue, the number of people and the date. Agencies and DMCs can request the agency rate card from the agencies page: it is sent as a PDF once they are signed up, with the terms and the commission in writing.
