Venues

Our spaces in the Penedès

We start with the two spaces we run directly, together with TeamTowers, and complete them with the partner network across the county.

Own space · TeamTowers

Cal Segue

At Cal Segue we rent the outdoor space: the tiled terrace, the covered porch with its barbecue and the garden, surrounded by vineyards with the hills behind. It is the place for a tasting, a human-tower workshop or a meal in the open air, for groups of up to around 30 people. The inside of the house is not for rent.

  • Barri Cusconar, Torrelles de Foix
  • Outdoor space only
  • Covered porch with barbecue
  • Up to 30 people

Own space · TeamTowers

The Castells Workshop

An open-air human-tower training ground adapted for corporate groups. It is the natural home of the «Fent Pinya» workshop: it runs rain or shine, with experienced casteller instructors and all safety equipment. The workshop is run by TeamTowers — full details at teamtowers.eu.

  • Open air
  • Groups of 11 to 50
  • Teaching and traditional equipment included
  • Expert instructors

Activities

Experiences that belong here

Our own activities and those of local partners, adapted to group size, language and objective.

Human-tower workshop «Fent Pinya»

Two hours to build a real human tower, with casteller instructors. The metaphor explains itself: base, trust, communication and a goal nobody reaches alone. A TeamTowers activity: teamtowers.eu.

Vineyard treasure hunt

A route in teams through the vineyards and paths of the Penedès, with challenges on collaboration, orientation and local knowledge.

Cooking challenge

A Masterchef-style format with local produce: teams cook, present and share. It ends at the table, which is where the day is really closed.

Cava and wine tasting

A guided tasting at the winery or the masia, on its own or as the closing of another activity. Available in Catalan, Spanish and English.

Outdoor activities

Walks, cycling, orienteering and team games in the county’s natural surroundings, with logistics and safety support.

DJ set

To close the day, the dinner or the company party. See Álvaro Solache’s profile.

Bespoke

If you have a specific goal — merging two teams, launching a project, closing a cycle — we design the activity from scratch.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask before booking

What area do you cover?

We work across the Alt Penedès and its surroundings, based in Vilafranca del Penedès. We are about 40 minutes from Barcelona and the airport, which makes a full company day out and back in the same day perfectly possible.

What kind of events do you produce?

Team days and team building, conventions and kick-offs, incentives, company celebrations and festivals. We work for event agencies, for venues that want a programme, and for end clients.

Do you have your own venue or work with others?

Both. We run Cal Segue, in Torrelles de Foix, and the Castells Workshop, both together with TeamTowers. Beyond that we work with a network of partner venues across the county: farmhouses, cava cellars and wineries open to visits, hotels with meeting rooms and natural settings.

How many people does Cal Segue take?

Groups of up to around 30 people. At Cal Segue we rent the outdoor space only: the tiled terrace, the covered porch with its barbecue and the garden. For larger groups we propose venues from the network.

What activities can we do?

The «Fent Pinya» human-tower workshop, vineyard treasure hunts, cooking challenges, cava and wine tastings, outdoor activities and a DJ set. We also design bespoke activities when there is a specific objective.

Do you work with event agencies?

Yes. We do executive production on site, venue scouting and booking, and white-label activities that the agency resells under its own brand.

What languages do you work in?

Catalan, Spanish and English, both in production and in the activities with participants.

How long does a quote take?

Usually between 48 and 72 working hours from the briefing, with venue options, programme and a fixed budget.
