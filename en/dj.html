No repertoire limits

I work with professional DJ streaming platforms, so the music is not limited to what sits on my hard drive. If someone asks for a track halfway through dinner, it plays two minutes later. And if the group is international, the set travels with it.

Prepared with you, not improvised

Before the event we agree the musical direction: what works, what does not, and what cannot be missing — with the event timing in front of us, because the reception, the dinner and two in the morning are not the same conversation.

Fits the production

I come from producing events and festivals, so I speak the rest of the crew’s language: AV, catering, protocol. I know when to pull back for a speech and when to push because the floor is cooling down.

Shows

A moment of the event with its own stage

Not everything is background music or a dance floor. Some moments call for a show: the group’s arrival, the toast, the close of the day.

Folk culture in the street

Gralla players and drums to welcome the group, walk them through the vineyards or kick off the party. The good kind of noise, and very local.

Human towers

A castells display or a workshop to join the pinya with a local colla. With gralla players alongside, it is the Penedès’ most recognisable scene.

See the activities

Family show

For days out with children: music and a show designed so the little ones can join in and the grown-ups enjoy it too.

Large format

A choir, cobla band or orchestra for a big celebration, a gala or a company anniversary. Planned well ahead, with the venue and the technical side in mind.

Sound and lighting

Equipment and technician, in the same running order

Most bands don’t bring their own PA, and a speech nobody can hear ruins a dinner. That is why the technical side is booked with the music, not afterwards.

Sound system

Sized to the venue and the audience: a barrel room is not the same as an open field among the vines.

Microphones for speeches

Microphones for welcomes, awards and presentations, with a mix that never swallows the voice.

Lighting

Ambient light for the venue and stage light for the live acts and the dance floor, for when the sun goes down.

Sound and lighting technician

One person in charge of the technical side throughout: set-up, sound check, changeovers between acts and get-out.

Coordination with the venue

Power supply, set-up times, volume limits and neighbours. We check it with the venue before it becomes a problem.

One person in charge

Someone who produces festivals coordinates the technical side: the DJ, the bands and the crew work to the same schedule and the same contact.

Production

Aura Open Air Festival

29, 30 and 31 May 2026 · Pontons (Barcelona)

I am the production lead of the Aura Open Air Festival. It is the most demanding school I know: schedules that cannot move, artists arriving late, weather, capacity, safety, and an audience that does not forgive a silence.

That experience is what later shows at a sixty-person company event, where the margin for error is the same but nobody sees it.
