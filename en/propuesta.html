What happens next

Four steps and no surprises

  1. We call you Within 24 working hours, to understand what doesn’t fit in a form.
  2. Tailored proposal, no price Programme, venues and experiences. Usually within 48–72 working hours.
  3. Budget Once the programme fits. Production is billed by the hour and services go at their price, with no commission on top.
  4. Booking The venue date is not held until the deposit. The proposal is valid for 30 days.
