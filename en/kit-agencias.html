What you can offer

Human-tower workshop «Fent Pinya»

Two hours to build a real human tower, with casteller instructors. The metaphor explains itself: base, trust, communication and a goal nobody reaches alone.

Cava and wine tasting

A guided tasting at the winery or the masia, on its own or as the closing of another activity. Available in Catalan, Spanish and English.

Olive oil tasting

Extra virgin olive oil tasting at an organic mill in L’Arboç, with the press right there and the award-winning oil made on site. It works all year round, unlike the grape harvest, and surprises even those who have already done three wine tastings.

Vineyard treasure hunt

A route in teams through the vineyards and paths of the Penedès, with challenges on collaboration, orientation and local knowledge.

Cooking challenge

A Masterchef-style format with local produce: teams cook, present and share. It ends at the table, which is where the day is really closed.

Outdoor activities

Walks, cycling, orienteering and team games in the county’s natural surroundings, with logistics and safety support.

Top-level chef

Signature cooking with local produce, at the farmhouse or wherever the event is held. A menu designed for the group and paired with the wines tasted that same day, not with a set menu written two years ago.

Public relations and protocol

For when the event has guests you cannot afford to overlook: press, institutions or the end client. Someone who knows who to greet first, and that an event is remembered by how people are received.

DJ for events

A DJ set backed by more than twenty years behind the decks. No repertoire limits, thanks to professional streaming platforms: the music follows the room in front of you, not what fits on a hard drive.

Nobody should drive back

A day in the Penedès means three or four stops: winery, lunch, activity and dinner. With a private minibus the group arrives together, nobody gets lost among the vines and the tasting is enjoyed in full. We pick up in Barcelona or at the airport and drop off at the hotel door.
